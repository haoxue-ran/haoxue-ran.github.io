  <section style="margin-bottom: 38px;">
    <h2 style="font-size: 1.45em; font-weight: 700; color: #0f172a; border-bottom: 2.5px solid #2563eb; padding-bottom: 8px; margin-bottom: 20px;">
      Research Interests
    </h2>
    
    <p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">
      My research lies at the dynamic interface of <span style="color: #1d4ed8; font-weight: 600;">Membrane Separation Technology</span> and <span style="color: #1d4ed8; font-weight: 600;">Environmental Data Science (AI/ML)</span>, bridging microscopic chemical mechanisms with data-driven predictive modeling. My key research pillars include:
    </p>

    <ul style="padding-left: 20px; margin: 0; line-height: 1.85;">
      <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">
        <strong style="color: #0f172a;">Fenton-Pretreatment & Interfacial Fouling Control:</strong> Developing trace-dose Fenton pretreatment technologies at neutral pH to mitigate organic fouling, with a focus on understanding the governing mechanisms of divalent metal ions (Ca<sup>2+</sup>, Mg<sup>2+</sup>).
      </li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">
        <strong style="color: #0f172a;">AI-Driven Causal Inference & Data-Driven Modeling:</strong> Integrating explainable machine learning with micro-interfacial analytical techniques to quantitatively decouple complex foulant-membrane causal interactions and predict membrane fouling behaviors.
      </li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">
        <strong style="color: #0f172a;">Multi-Scale Computational Simulations:</strong> Combining Molecular Dynamics (MD) simulations, Density Functional Theory (DFT), and artificial intelligence to gain atomic-level insights into interfacial crystallization and foulant transport dynamics.
      </li>
    </ul>
  </section>
