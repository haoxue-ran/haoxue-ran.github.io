## Biography

I am Haoxue Ran, a Ph.D. candidate in Environmental Science and Engineering at the School of Environmental and Municipal Engineering, **Xi'an University of Architecture and Technology (XAUAT)**. I conduct my research under the supervision of **Prof. Rui Miao** and collaborate closely with **Associate Researcher Jin Zhao** at Xi'an Jiaotong University. 

My expected graduation date is **June 2027**. I am actively seeking **Postdoctoral Positions (starting in Fall 2027)** in advanced water treatment, membrane separation technology, and data-driven environmental science.

## Research Interests

My research lies at the dynamic interface of **Membrane Separation Technology** and **Environmental Data Science (AI/ML)**, bridging microscopic chemical mechanisms with data-driven predictive modeling. My key research pillars include:

* **Fenton-Pretreatment & Interfacial Fouling Control**: Developing trace-dose Fenton pretreatment technologies at neutral pH to mitigate organic fouling, with a focus on understanding the governing mechanisms of divalent metal ions ($\text{Ca}^{2+}$, $\text{Mg}^{2+}$).
* **AI-Driven Causal Inference & Data-Driven Modeling**: Integrating explainable machine learning with micro-interfacial analytical techniques to quantitatively decouple complex foulant-membrane causal interactions and predict membrane fouling behaviors.
* **Multi-Scale Computational Simulations**: Combining Molecular Dynamics (MD) simulations, Density Functional Theory (DFT), and artificial intelligence to gain atomic-level insights into interfacial crystallization and foulant transport dynamics.
