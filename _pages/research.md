  <h2 class="hr-section-title">Research Directions</h2>
  <div class="hr-research-grid">
    <article class="hr-research-card"><span class="hr-card-index">01 · PROCESS</span><h3>Trace-Fenton Pretreatment &amp; Fouling Control</h3><p>Developing neutral-pH, trace-dose Fenton strategies to mitigate organic membrane fouling and resolving the roles of foulant transformation, membrane adhesion, and divalent cations.</p><p class="hr-card-evidence"><strong>Key contribution:</strong> Connected pretreatment chemistry with interfacial-force measurements and filtration performance.</p></article>
    <article class="hr-research-card"><span class="hr-card-index">02 · INFERENCE</span><h3>Predictive Modeling &amp; Causal Inference</h3><p>Building interpretable machine-learning frameworks to predict membrane flux, identify governing variables, and quantify the contributions of individual causal pathways.</p><p class="hr-card-evidence"><strong>Key contribution:</strong> Moved beyond feature importance toward mechanism-oriented causal attribution.</p></article>
    <article class="hr-research-card"><span class="hr-card-index">03 · MOLECULAR SCALE</span><h3>MD Simulation &amp; AI-Assisted Discovery</h3><p>Integrating molecular dynamics simulations with artificial intelligence to examine Ca<sup>2+</sup>/Mg<sup>2+</sup>-mediated interfacial behavior and nonlinear dose-response relationships.</p><p class="hr-card-evidence"><strong>Current focus:</strong> Linking atomic-scale interactions to experimentally observed fouling mitigation.</p></article>
  </div>
