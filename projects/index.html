Projects

Research directions, funded projects, and collaborative work from my doctoral research.

research directions

Membrane Fouling Control

Developing trace-dose Fenton pretreatment strategies at neutral pH and quantifying interfacial mechanisms that govern organic membrane fouling.

Machine Learning & Causal Inference

Using interpretable ML, feature selection, explainable AI, DAGs, and causal inference to predict fouling behavior and identify key governing factors.

Molecular Simulation

Applying molecular dynamics simulations to analyze Ca2+/Mg2+ interactions and the microscopic mechanisms of Fenton-pretreated membrane fouling.

Resource Recovery

Studying interfacial migration of salt ions in brine and membrane/adsorbent fouling prevention for valuable resource extraction from salt-lake brine.
